3. Certain persons not to undertake biodiversity-related activities without approval of National Biodiversity Authority.--
(1) No person referred to in sub-section (2) shall, without previous approval of the National Biodiversity Authority, obtain any biological resource occurring in India or knowledge associated thereto for research or for commercial utilisation or for bio-survey and bio-utilisation.
(2) The persons who shall be required to take the approval of the National Biodiversity Authority under sub-section (1) are the following, namely:--
(a) a person who is not a citizen of India;
(b) a citizen of India, who is a non-resident as defined in clause (30) of section 2 of the Income-tax Act, 1961;
(c) a body corporate, association or organisation--
(i) not incorporated or registered in India; or
(ii) incorporated or registered in India under any law for the time being in force, which is controlled by a foreigner within the meaning of clause (27) of section 2 of the Companies Act, 2013.

4. Results of research not to be transferred to certain persons without approval of National Biodiversity Authority.--
No person or entity shall share or transfer any result of the research on any biological resource occurring in, or obtained or accessed from, India or traditional knowledge associated thereto, for monetary consideration or otherwise, to a person or body corporate referred to in sub-section (2) of section 3, without the prior written approval of the National Biodiversity Authority, except the codified traditional knowledge which is only for Indians:
Provided that this section shall not apply if publication of research papers or dissemination of knowledge in any seminar or workshop involving financial benefit is as per guidelines issued by the Central Government:
Provided further that where the results of research are used for further research, registration with the National Biodiversity Authority shall be necessary:
Provided also that if the results of research are used for commercial utilisation or for obtaining any intellectual property rights, within or outside India, prior approval of the National Biodiversity Authority shall be required in accordance with this Act.

5. Certain provisions not to apply to certain collaborative research projects.--
(1) Sections 3 and 4 shall not apply to collaborative research projects involving transfer or exchange of biological resource or traditional knowledge associated thereto between institutions, including Government-sponsored institutions of India, and institutions in other countries, if such projects satisfy the conditions in sub-section (3).
(2) Collaborative research projects other than those in sub-section (1), based on agreements concluded before commencement of this Act and still in force, shall, to the extent inconsistent with this Act or guidelines under clause (a) of sub-section (3), be void.
(3) For the purposes of sub-section (1), collaborative research projects shall--
(a) conform to policy guidelines issued by the Central Government;
(b) be approved by the Central Government.

6. Application for intellectual property rights not to be made without approval of National Biodiversity Authority.--
(1) Any person or entity covered under sub-section (2) of section 3, applying for an intellectual property right (by whatever name called) in or outside India, for any invention based on any research or information on a biological resource accessed from India (including biological resources deposited in repositories outside India) or associated traditional knowledge, shall obtain prior approval of the National Biodiversity Authority before grant of such intellectual property right.
(1A) Any person covered under section 7 applying for such an intellectual property right shall register with the National Biodiversity Authority before grant of the right.
(1B) Any person covered under section 7 who has already obtained such an intellectual property right shall obtain prior approval of the National Biodiversity Authority at the time of commercialisation.
(2) The National Biodiversity Authority may, while granting approval, impose a benefit-sharing fee or royalty, or both, or other conditions including sharing of financial benefits from commercial utilisation of the right.
(3) This section does not apply to an application for any right under a law relating to protection of plant varieties.
(4) Where a right is granted under the law referred to in sub-section (3), the granting authority shall endorse a copy of the grant document to the National Biodiversity Authority.

7. Prior intimation to State Biodiversity Board for accessing biological resource for certain purposes.--
(1) No person, other than a person covered under sub-section (2) of section 3, shall access any biological resource and its associated knowledge for commercial utilisation without giving prior intimation to the concerned State Biodiversity Board (subject to clause (b) of section 23 and sub-section (2) of section 24):
Provided that this section does not apply to codified traditional knowledge, cultivated medicinal plants and their products, local people and communities of the area (including growers and cultivators of biodiversity), and to vaids, hakims and registered AYUSH practitioners who have been practising indigenous medicine, including Indian systems of medicine, as a profession for sustenance and livelihood.
(2) In the case of cultivated medicinal plants, the exemption under sub-section (1) is available only if a certificate of origin is obtained from the Biodiversity Management Committee, in the manner prescribed.
(3) The Biodiversity Management Committee shall, based on entries in prescribed books maintained in the prescribed manner, issue the certificate of origin under sub-section (2) in the prescribed manner.

Practical read for the PIP/ABS module: an Ayurvedic MSME whose formulation is (a) based on codified/classical texts, or (b) uses cultivated (not wild-collected) medicinal plants with a Biodiversity Management Committee certificate of origin, and is run by growers/cultivators or registered AYUSH practitioners, sits inside the Section 7(1) proviso exemption and only needs the lighter "prior intimation" track, not full Section 3 NBA approval. A formulation using wild-sourced biological material, or run by a foreign-controlled entity or non-resident, falls under Section 3 and needs prior NBA approval before research, commercial utilisation, or IP filing (Section 6).
