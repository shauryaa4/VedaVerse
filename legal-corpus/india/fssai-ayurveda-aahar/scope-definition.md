“Ayurveda Aahara” means a food prepared in accordance with the recipes or ingredients or
processes as per method described in the authoritative books of Ayurveda listed under ‘Schedule A’
of these regulations including products which have other botanical ingredients in accordance with
the concept of Ayurveda Aahara but does not include Ayurvedic drugs or proprietary Ayurvedic
medicines and medicinal products, cosmetics, narcotic or psychotropic substances, herbs listed under
Schedule E-1 of Drug and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and the Drug and Cosmetics Rules, 1945, metals
based Ayurvedic drugs or medicines, bhasma or pishti and any other ingredients notified by the
Authority from time to time.
